# ScreenVNC

Control your Mac from your iPhone or iPad.

## What is ScreenVNC?

ScreenVNC is a Mac-first VNC viewer for macOS Screen Sharing. Connect over your local network or a VPN and see your desktop right on your device. Then take control with natural touch gestures and a full keyboard.

## Features

**BUILT FOR YOUR MAC**

- Automatically finds nearby Macs on your network, so there is nothing to type.
- Sign in with your Mac username and password, or with a VNC password.
- Multiple displays are supported, so every screen on your Mac is reachable.

**NATURAL TOUCH CONTROLS**

- Tap to click and long-press to right-click.
- Drag to move the pointer, or switch to trackpad-style relative mode.
- Two-finger drag to scroll and pinch to zoom.
- Full keyboard with Command, Option, and Control keys for shortcuts.

**FAST AND SMOOTH**

- Repaints only the parts of the screen that change, so scrolling and typing stay smooth.
- Low latency keeps the pointer and keyboard feeling responsive, even on busy networks.
- Adaptive quality automatically balances sharpness and speed to match your connection.

**YOUR CONNECTIONS, EVERYWHERE**

- Save each Mac as a profile with its own display settings, ready to reuse.
- Sync your saved profiles through your iCloud account.
- Lock the app, or individual connections, with Face ID or Touch ID.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**

- Credentials stay on your device and are never uploaded.
- No tracking, no analytics, and no account required.
- iCloud profile backups carry settings only, never passwords.

## Getting started

1. On your Mac, open System Settings, go to General, then Sharing, and turn on Screen Sharing.
2. Install ScreenVNC on your iPhone or iPad, then pick your Mac; nearby Macs appear automatically.
3. Sign in with the same Mac username and password you use every day, and take control.

## Touch and keyboard controls

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Tap | Click |
| Long-press | Right-click |
| Two-finger drag | Scroll |
| Drag | Move the pointer |
| Pinch | Zoom in and out |
| Keyboard bar | Type plus Command, Option, and Control |

## Requirements

- A Mac with Screen Sharing enabled.
- An iPhone or iPad.
- Both devices on the same network, or connected through a VPN while you are away.
